Research profile

Neural networks.
Optical systems.
Experimental control.

I am a Master's candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Henan University of Technology, ranked first in my cohort. My research combines neural-network modeling, industrial imaging, optomechatronic prototyping, embedded control, and reproducible experimental evaluation.

I led an end-to-end mechanical–vision platform integrating controlled image acquisition, lightweight neural networks, STM32 communication, Python visualization, and CPU-only deployment. I also contributed to the experimental validation of an ultrasonic-motor-actuated PDMS flexible zoom lens published in Ultrasonics.

For doctoral training, I aim to study the modeling, training, and experimental implementation of all-optical neural networks and photonic information-processing systems. My current work provides a foundation in both computational model development and hardware-aware experimentation; optical computing is the direction I now seek to develop rigorously.
