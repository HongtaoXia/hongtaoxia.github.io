Research profile

Methods first.
Applications grounded in evidence.

I am a Master's candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Henan University of Technology, ranked first in my cohort. My graduate research combines computer vision, dataset curation, controlled experimentation, embedded control, and edge deployment.

My work on small, irregular, and partially occluded targets provides a transferable technical foundation for cell identification, semantic segmentation, and morphology-aware analysis in biomedical imaging. I am particularly interested in research that connects rigorous image analysis with clinically meaningful questions.

Selected research

From curated data to deployed systems

Representative work demonstrating experimental design, quantitative validation, and end-to-end implementation.

01

Computer vision · Project lead

Continuous-flow wheat impurity analysis

Led the development of a mechanical–visual inspection system integrating controlled feeding, industrial imaging, lightweight object detection, quantitative multi-speed validation, and CPU-only deployment.

  • Curated 2,400 images with 6,618 instances across four visually heterogeneous classes.
  • Developed BG-DFL for improved localization of small, irregular targets.
  • Validated six YOLO architectures with three random seeds and controlled flow experiments.
